🌱 EcoWatch
Report a garbage problem. Follow the cleanup. Find your next pickup.

EcoWatch helps people in Surigao City check garbage pickup schedules and track
cleanup reports from the first report to the final check.

Report a problem · Check garbage pickup · Accounts and roles
Report a problem
Example. You see a pile of garbage near a street corner. Here’s what happens
after you report it.
Step	What happens	Report status
1. Report it	A resident or barangay official opens Report a Problem, chooses a category and barangay, types the location, and drops a map pin. A description and photo are optional.	Pending
2. Assign it	The City General Services Office (CGSO) admin opens Reports and assigns a collector.	Assigned
3. Clean it	The collector opens My Work and clicks Start Cleanup.	In Progress
4. Send proof	The collector uploads a before photo and an after photo, then clicks Submit for Verification.	For Verification
5. Check it	The CGSO admin checks both photos and clicks Approve Cleanup.	Resolved
6. Close it	The person who reported it opens My Reports → View and clicks Yes, Resolved after approval.	Closed


<details>
<summary><strong>What if the garbage is still there?</strong></summary>- The admin can Reject Cleanup with a reason. The report becomes
  Reopened, and the collector can resume the cleanup and send new photos.
- After approval, the person who reported it can click No, Still Not Resolved.
  The status becomes Not Resolved. The admin can review the feedback and
  reopen the report if more cleanup is needed.
- An answer sent before approval is saved as feedback. Closing still requires
  admin approval and a confirmation from the person who reported it.
</details>Check garbage pickup
On Home, open Schedule to find the pickup day and exact time for your
barangay, such as Monday at 7:00 AM. Use Collection Routes to see the
assigned truck and route on the map. Read Announcements for public updates.
<details>
<summary><strong>How the admin and collector run pickup</strong></summary>1. The admin sets up the truck and driver in Trucks, then uses
   Route Assignment to set the barangay, streets, and first pickup schedule.
   The admin can change pickup days or times in Schedules.
2. On pickup day, the collector opens My Work and marks each area
   Pending → Collecting → Done. Truck maintenance blocks progress updates
   and shows a service interruption on the public schedule.
3. The collector can send a note and an optional photo about a delay or blocked
   road. The admin reads it under Collection Monitoring.
</details>Accounts and roles
- Residents can register and sign in to submit and track their reports.
- Barangay officials can also see other reports and collection progress in
  their barangay.
- Collectors use My Work for assigned reports and regular pickup.
- CGSO admins use the Dashboard to see what needs attention and
  Users & Barangays to create collector and official accounts.
Use Forgot Password on the login page to request a reset link. After signing
in, use My Account to update your details or password.
